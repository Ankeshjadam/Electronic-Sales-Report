ELECTRONIC SALES REPORT
Financial Year 2024 & 2025 - Detailed Insights

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
   This report presents a detailed analysis of Electronic Sales for the years 2024 and 2025. The business has shown consistent growth. Total revenue increased from ₹1.06 Billion in 2024 to ₹1.09 Billion in 2025, with a profit growth of 3.34%. The average discount has been maintained at 4.87% in both years, which indicates strong pricing control.

3. DETAILED ANALYSIS
   A. Monthly Sales Trend (Sum of Net Sales):-In 2024: The highest sale was recorded in May (approx. ₹110M). There was a sharp fall in June (₹80M), but recovery was seen in Q4.In 2025: The sales trend is more volatile. Peaks were observed in April, July and September (above ₹100M). A major drop was observed in October 2025. This suggests that the seasonality pattern has shifted compared to 2024.
   B. Region-wise Performance:-Sales are well-balanced across all five regions. In 2025, West (20.79%) and South (20.36%) are the top contributing regions, followed by Central (19.96%) and North (19.76%). No region is underperforming significantly, which shows good pan-India distribution.
   C. Payment Method Analysis:-Customer payment preference is equally divided, which is a healthy sign. In 2025, Net Banking and EMI contribute 19% and 17% respectively. UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card and Cash are all in the range of 15% to 17%. This indicates that customers are comfortable with all digital and cash modes.
   D. Brand-wise Performance:-Apple is the undisputed market leader. In 2024, Apple generated ₹0.33bn sales, and in 2025, ₹0.32bn. Samsung is at second position with approx. ₹0.21bn - ₹0.23bn. Brands like Boat, JBL, and Logitech are at the bottom. The company should continue to focus on premium brands like Apple and Samsung.
   E. Category-wise and Customer Type:-By Category: Laptops and Smartphones are the highest-selling categories in terms of quantity, followed by Televisions and Audio products. Networking and Accessories have the lowest demand. By Customer Type: Corporate (34.79% in 2025), Retail (33.64%) and Online (31.57%) segments are almost equally balanced. Corporate segment has slightly increased from 2024 to 2025.

4. KEY FINDINGS & RECOMMENDATIONS
   Maintain Premium Stock: As Apple and Samsung drive maximum revenue, ensure their availability, especially in North and West regions. Investigate October Dip: The sharp drop in October 2025 needs to be investigated. It may be due to stock-out or festive competition. Marketing Strategy: Since UPI and EMI are growing, introduce special offers on these payment modes to increase conversion. Focus on Corporate Clients: The Corporate segment gives the highest quantity. B2B offers and bulk discounts can be planned for this segment.

5. CONCLUSION
   The overall business performance from 2024 to 2025 is positive and stable. The increase in total revenue and profit with a controlled discount rate shows that the business is moving in the right direction. With a more stable monthly sales plan and focus on top brands, the company can target ₹1.20bn in the next year.
